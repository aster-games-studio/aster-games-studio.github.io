Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The King's Square ("the game") is a chess game developed by Aster Games Studio. This page explains what data the game processes, why, and what your rights are.

In short: the game has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your games, statistics and settings stay on your device. Data only leaves the device when you send feedback, rate the game or play online.

1. Data that stays on your device

Saved games, statistics, the player names you create, solved puzzles and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. They are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

2. "Send feedback" and "Rate the game"

Only when you send feedback or a rating, the game sends:

  • your message (and the star rating, if any) and the chosen category;
  • the game version, the platform (Android, Windows) and the device model (for example "samsung SM-S928B");
  • the position of the current game (FEN notation), only if you choose to attach it;
  • a random installation identifier, created by the game, which cannot identify you; it is used to group feedback from the same device and to avoid duplicates.

This data is only used to fix problems and improve the game. It is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored in the developer's private Google Drive, through a Google Apps Script. Please do not write personal information (name, address, phone number…) in your messages. Feedback is kept as long as it is useful to improve the game, and at most 2 years.

3. Online play

To play over the Internet, the game uses services from Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services):

  • anonymous sign-in: Unity assigns the device an anonymous player ID, with no account, e-mail or password;
  • matchmaking (Lobby) and network relay (Relay): to create or join a game, the player name you chose in the game is used as the game's name and sent to your opponent; your moves go through Unity's servers during the game.

Like any online service, Unity also receives the device's IP address. We do not keep any of this data. Unity processes it under its own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Local network play connects two devices on the same Wi-Fi network directly, without going through our servers or Unity's.

4. Updates

  • Google Play version: the game asks Google Play whether a new version exists and downloads it through Google Play. These exchanges are governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Version installed from an APK file: the game regularly asks our Google script (HTTPS) for the latest published version; no personal data is sent.

5. Notifications and permissions

The game may ask for permission to show notifications (daily puzzle reminder and, for the APK version, new version available). These notifications are scheduled on the device; no data is sent to show them. The game also uses Internet access and vibration.

6. What the game does not do

The game shows no ads, uses no analytics or advertising tracking, sells no data and shares no data for commercial purposes. It does not ask for your real name, your e-mail address or your location.

7. Children

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in feedback, contact us so that we can delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to aster.games.studio@gmail.com. As we do not know your identity, please give the approximate date and the content of the feedback concerned so that we can find it. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

9. Changes

This policy may be updated, for example when new features are added. The date at the top of the page shows the last change.
