Contact

The King's Square is developed by Aster Games Studio.

E-mail: aster.games.studio@gmail.com

The quickest way to report a problem or suggest an idea is the Send feedback button in the game menu. It automatically includes the game version and the device model, and you can attach the current position. Every message is read.

For a problem, please mention if you can:

  • the model of your phone or tablet;
  • the game version (Menu > What's new);
  • what you were doing and what happened.

We reply in English or French, usually within a few days.
