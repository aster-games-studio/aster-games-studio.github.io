Help and FAQ

Start a game

Open the ☰ Menu, then New game. Choose the game mode (against the computer, two players on one screen, or AI versus AI), your colour, the computer level and the clock. To play, tap a piece then its target square, or drag it.

Which level should I choose?

Five levels: 1. Beginner, 2. Easy, 3. Intermediate, 4. Advanced, 5. Strong. Start at level 2 or 3 and move up once you win regularly. The game also estimates your rating from your games against the computer finished without "Undo" (Menu > Statistics).

I'm a beginner: how do I learn the rules?

Menu > Learn to play: 13 short lessons, from the rook to stalemate. Each lesson explains a rule, then offers exercises on the board; a wrong move shows a tip. Completed lessons are ticked and can be replayed.

The daily puzzle

A new puzzle every day: solving it day after day grows your streak. A daily reminder at 7 pm can be turned on in Options ("Daily puzzle reminder (7 pm)"). More puzzles are in Menu > Puzzles, by theme and difficulty.

Practise openings

Menu > Opening training: pick one of 12 openings and play its main line move by move, with the computer playing the other side. Hint shows the expected move. At the end of the line you see your score and your best, and you can keep playing against the computer.

Play a friend online

Menu > Play online. One player chooses "Create a game (code to share)" and sends the code; the other enters it in "Join". "Quick match" pairs you with a random opponent. At home you can also play over the local network (IP): both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi. A game cut off by a lost connection can resume.

Review and analyse a game

At the end of a game, Review game: the computer analyses every move, flags inaccuracies, mistakes and blunders and explains each one in a sentence (piece left undefended, fork, pin, missed or allowed mate…), with the best move, the advantage graph and each player's accuracy. Analyze opens the analysis view (evaluation, best move, suggested line). Every finished game is saved so you can review or reload it later.

Achievements

Menu > Achievements: 28 goals (first win, puzzles solved, streaks, challenges…), stored on your device only, with no account. Games count from the starting position and without taking back moves.

Save, load, copy a position

Menu > Save and Load use the PGN format, readable by every chess program. Copy FEN copies the current position.

Useful settings

In Options: board theme (classic wood, tournament green, marble, slate, high contrast…), piece style (Classic, Neo, Staunton wood, High contrast), French (C, F, T, D, R) or English (N, B, R, Q, K) notation, coordinates around the board, sound and volume, vibration, live rating of your moves, language and players.

Several players on one device

Options > Players…: each player has their own statistics, achievements and estimated rating.

Are my games kept?

Yes, on your device only: there is no account. They survive updates but are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data. To keep a game, save it as PGN.

A problem, an idea?

The Send feedback button (Menu) sends your message directly, with the game version; you can attach the current position. You can also write to us: see the Contact page.
