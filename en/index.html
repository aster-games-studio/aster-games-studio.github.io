An elegant, complete chess game

The King's Square is a chess game for Android, built to help you improve at your own pace. Sit down at a walnut table, in front of a wooden, marble or slate board, and play.

What you'll find in the game

  • Play the computer: five levels, from Beginner to Strong, even offline, with the clock of your choice.
  • Learn to play: 13 short lessons with exercises on the board, from how the pieces move to stalemate.
  • Chess puzzles: mate in 1, 2 or 3, winning material, and a new daily puzzle every day.
  • Opening training: 12 classic openings to replay move by move, with the idea behind each move.
  • Online or with a friend: share a game code with a friend, play over your local network, or two players on one screen.
  • Improve: a game review that explains every mistake, advantage graph, accuracy and an analysis view.
  • Achievements and stats: 28 achievements to unlock, full history, per-player statistics, PGN and FEN.
  • Your way: several board themes and four piece styles, including a high-contrast style.

No account, no ads, no tracking: your games and statistics stay on your device.
